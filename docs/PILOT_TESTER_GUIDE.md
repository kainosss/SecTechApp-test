# Fieldwise pilot — tester guide

Thanks for helping test Fieldwise. This takes about 30–45 minutes.

## What Fieldwise is
A phone-friendly toolkit for electronic security technicians:
- **Device Library** — look up panels, detectors and readers.
- **EOL Wiring Assistant** — end-of-line resistor values and the readings a zone should give.
- **Circuit Lab** — practise wiring a detector and measuring it with a virtual multimeter.
- **Fault Finder** — step-by-step help narrowing down a fault.
- **Technical Docs** — a register of a site's equipment.
- **Service Reports** — record a job, then create a client PDF or an Excel report.

It is a **test version**. Technical information may be incomplete or unverified — the manufacturer's documentation
always comes first. Check anything before you apply it to a live system.

## Opening the pilot
Use the web address and access password the pilot coordinator sent you (they arrive separately). Open the address
in **Chrome** on Android or **Safari** on iPhone, enter the password, and you'll see the Fieldwise dashboard.

## Install it on your phone (optional)
- **Android (Chrome):** tap ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Then open Fieldwise from its icon. Menu names differ between phones and browsers. The app still needs internet to
open. On iPhone, the home-screen app may keep its records separately from Safari — pick one and stick with it.

## Explore the demo
On the dashboard tap **Explore the demo site** (or Help → Demo site). Riverside Business Centre is a **fictional**
site with CCTV, an alarm and access control. A yellow **Demo workspace** banner shows while you're in it; your own
records are hidden and untouched. Use **Reset demo…** to start over and **Exit demo** to leave.

## Five tasks
1. **Find a device** — search for a model you know and open it. Is the information clear?
2. **Check a zone reading** — in the EOL Wiring Assistant, try the 2k2/4k4 example and switch between sealed, alarm,
   tamper, cut cable and short. Do the readings make sense?
3. **Do a Circuit Lab exercise** — wire the detector on the Wiring bench until it's marked correct, and use the
   meter.
4. **Write up a demo job** — in the demo site's register, start a service report under "Service reports for this
   site", add a few cameras from the site, record a fault, then create the client PDF and the Excel report.
5. **Back up** — exit the demo, create a small register with made-up details, then Technical Docs → **Download
   backup**. If you can, restore it on another browser or phone (**Restore from file…** → Import).

## Send feedback
Open **Help → Write feedback** (or Feedback in the side menu on a computer). Choose the type, fill in what's useful,
then **Copy feedback** or **Download as a file** and send it to the pilot coordinator. Fieldwise never sends anything
itself and never attaches your records or screenshots. Please report anything wrong, confusing, slow or hard to tap
— especially incorrect technical information.

## Exporting a backup
Technical Docs or Service Reports → **Backup & restore** (bottom of the page) → **Download backup**. Keep the file somewhere safe. It contains your
registers, reports and branding. (Backup is switched off inside the demo.)

## Known limitations
- Records are saved **in this browser on this phone only**. They don't sync to other devices or browsers.
- Clearing browser data, private browsing, or long periods without use (iPhone Safari) can delete records — back up.
- No offline mode: you need a connection to open Fieldwise.
- Most technical content is marked **Requires verification**; nothing replaces the manufacturer's manual.
- Installation and downloads behave differently between phones and browser versions.

## Please don't enter
- Real customer names, addresses or site details
- Real IP addresses, network details or serial numbers
- Passwords, alarm codes, user codes or other credentials
- Anything you wouldn't want on a test system

Use the demo site or made-up details instead.
